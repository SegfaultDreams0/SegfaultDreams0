## About
 
I am interested in the intersection of mathematics, physics, engineering, and computational science. My work revolves around mathematical modelling, numerical methods, system architecture, and the computational exploration of complex physical and geometric systems, all built on **Roblox** in **Luau**.
 
## What I Work On
 
- Computational mathematics libraries and numerical algorithms, implemented in Luau on Roblox
- Geometric transformations, Lie groups, and advanced kinematics
- Modelling mathematical and physical systems through computational methods
- Modular software architectures and engineering tools
- Robotics concepts, trajectory generation, and motion systems
- Reverse engineering, cybersecurity, and software internals
## Selected Project
 
### VectorForge
 
An experimental computational mathematics library for Roblox Luau, covering:
 
- Lie group kinematics
- Spectral linear algebra
- Bayesian state estimation
- Differential path geometry
An ongoing exploration of mathematical methods and their practical implementation in a real-time engine.
